1. What the PRO plan includes

The PRO plan unlocks in Larky, among other things:

  • the full wake-up check (a series of checks after you turn the alarm off),
  • the “Steps” mission,
  • up to five missions per alarm (three in FREE),
  • Deep Sleep (bedtime in sleep cycles and an evening reminder),
  • 30 and 180-day statistics (7 days in FREE).

The current list of PRO features is always shown in the app, on the PRO screen, before you buy. Features may evolve; we don't take PRO features away during a period you have already paid for.

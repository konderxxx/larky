Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026
App: Larky (Android, com.sonntech.larky)
Data controller: Sonntech Maciej Gierłachowski
Contact: konderdev@gmail.com

Larky is an alarm clock. It needs no account and no personal data to work — every alarm feature runs without signing in and without an internet connection.

If you turn on cloud sync, we store only what is needed for your wake-up streak, achievements and sleep history to survive a change of phone. We do not sell data, show ads, or use any analytics or tracking tools.

Photos taken in the photo mission never leave your phone — they are checked locally, by a model that runs offline.

What data we collect

Data that stays on your phone only

It never reaches any server and disappears when you uninstall the app:

  • alarms (times, days, names, mission settings, chosen sounds),
  • photos used as alarm backgrounds and photos taken in the photo mission,
  • barcodes and QR codes saved for the "Scan a code" mission,
  • alarm session history (how you dismissed the alarm, mission times),
  • app settings.

Data stored in the cloud (only with sync enabled)

  • Account identifier — a random technical identifier (UUID) created automatically. The account is anonymous: it contains no name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Device time zone — needed so the server can determine the date of your wake-up correctly (otherwise your streak would break when travelling).
  • Wake-ups — the date and time you successfully dismissed an alarm and which alarm it was. This is what the streak and statistics are calculated from.
  • Achievements — how many missions of each type you played, your best reaction time, and unlocked badges with their unlock dates.
  • Sleep history — if you use the "Going to sleep" mode: when you went to bed and got up, your planned bedtime, and how long you slept.
  • Mission templates you publish — the name and mission configuration, plus your votes on other people's templates. Publishing is voluntary, and we strip references to your photos and codes before sending.

Data from your Google account (only if you link it)

Linking a Google account is optional and serves one purpose: recovering your data after a change of phone. We then receive your e-mail address, the name shown in your Google profile, and the address of your profile picture. We have no access to your mail, contacts, files or any other Google service.

What we do NOT collect

Location, contacts, the list of installed apps, audio recordings, photos, advertising identifiers. We use no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

Why we process this data, and on what legal basis

We process cloud data to deliver the features you ask for: syncing your streak, achievements and sleep history across devices and recovering them after a change of phone. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) — without this data the feature simply does not work.

Publishing a mission template to the community is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by deliberately publishing the template. You can withdraw it by deleting the template in the app.

App permissions

  • Alarms and reminders, exact alarms — so the alarm rings at the right time.
  • Notifications — the alarm itself, the sleep countdown, routine reminders.
  • Display over other apps, full-screen intent — so the ringing screen appears on a locked phone.
  • Camera — only in the "Scan a code" and photo missions. The image is analysed on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Motion sensors — the "Shake" and "Level" missions.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Data is stored on servers in the European Union and transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) only.
  • Google — if you use Google sign-in, Google confirms your identity under its own privacy policy.

We share data with no one else and we do not sell it.

How long we keep data

Cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists. Raw mission events (used to award achievements) are deleted automatically after 90 days — only counters and unlocked badges remain. When you delete your account, all your cloud data disappears immediately and irreversibly.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, port it and object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

In practice, you can exercise the most important ones in the app:

  • Turn off sync — Settings → Privacy. From that moment nothing leaves your phone.
  • Delete your account and all its data — the Account tab, at the bottom, "Danger zone" section. Details: account deletion.
  • Delete a published template — the Community templates screen.

You can also write to the contact address above — we reply within 30 days at the latest.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with data, write to the contact address and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will announce material changes in the app or in the Google Play listing. The date of the last update is shown at the top.
